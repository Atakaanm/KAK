  Karanlık bir zindanın ortasındasın ve dört köşedeki muhafızlar sana taş fırlatıyor.

  - Oyun sırasında topladığın altınla yeni kahraman ve pet aç
  - Piksel sanatı, meşale ışığında bir zindan
  - İnternet gerekmez, hesap gerekmez

  Rekorunu kır, arkadaşlarına meydan oku. Kaç, Ata, kaç!

## English
- **Name (30):** Kaç Ata Kaç — Rock Dodge
- **Subtitle, App Store (30):** Survive the endless rock storm
- **Short description, Google Play (80):** Rocks from every corner! Dodge, dash, pick your hero and beat your best.
- **Promotional text, App Store (170):** Dodge with one thumb, graze rocks for bonus points and grab gold. 5 heroes, loyal pets, missions and daily rewards make every run a little more exciting!
- **Description (4000):**

  You're trapped in a dark dungeon and four guards are hurling rocks at you.
  Dodge with one thumb, dash at the perfect moment and graze rocks for bonus points.
  The longer you survive, the wilder it gets: pebbles, boulders, bouncing rocks, splitting rocks, meteors…

  **Features**
  - One-handed, portrait, fast-paced arcade action
  - 7 rock types, 4 surprise events (Rock Rain, Crossfire, Silence, Rolling Boulder)
  - Close-call bonus and combo multiplier
  - Shield, speed, slow-mo, ghost and extra-life power-ups
  - 5 heroes with their own style: balanced Ata and Ada, speedy Swift, tough Tank, gold-hungry Lucky
  - Pets: a Firefly that pulls gold to you and a Turtle that shields you now and then
  - New missions every run and a 7-day reward streak
  - Spend the gold you collect to unlock new heroes and pets
  - Pixel art dungeon lit by torches
  - Plays offline, no account needed

  Beat your best and challenge your friends. Run, Ata, run!
