Son güncelleme: 2026-09-26

# Privacy Policy — Kaç Ata Kaç

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Kaç Ata Kaç does **not** collect, share or sell personal data.

- Game progress (best score, stats, gold, unlocked heroes and pets, missions, daily reward streak, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to any server.
- There are no accounts, analytics, online features or in-app purchases. This version shows no ads.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all data; you can also use "Reset Progress" in Settings.
- The game does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (it collects no data from anyone).

If ads or any other service are added, this policy will be updated before that change is released.
