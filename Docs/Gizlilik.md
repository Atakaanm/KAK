# Privacy Policy (draft) — Kaç Ata Kaç

Kaç Ata Kaç does **not** collect, share or sell personal data. Game progress (best score, stats, settings) is stored only on your device and never sent to any server. There are no ads, analytics, accounts or online features. Uninstalling the app deletes all data; you can also use "Reset Progress" in Settings.
